# Contact globe correction

The globe now uses substantially thicker, varied-width amber conduits with animated banding, moving highlights, soft depth-tested halos and nine-part pulse trails. Graphite continent plates, denser metallic dot fields, procedural surface grain, brighter neutral/amber fill and a restrained atmospheric rim give the sphere visible structure. Ottawa has a local light and glow anchor. Controlled energy motes follow the route environment: 110 desktop / 36 mobile. The final refinement softened halo edges and smoothed the thickest routes.

Six desktop paths / three mobile paths remain depth-occluded by the sphere. Shared materials, instanced land/nodes/pulses, one points draw, adaptive DPR/bloom and the existing offscreen budget remain in use. No shadows, new renderer, heavy globe asset or routing change. Desktop sample: 37 calls including bloom, 59,719 triangles; mobile: 16 calls, 27,232 triangles. This is a richer but bounded scene; software-browser samples do not establish hardware frame rate.

Reduced motion preserves the new materials, surface detail, glow and fixed pulse composition, with no continuous clock or pointer reaction. Mobile keeps thick routes and lit surface detail without bloom or the two traveling lights. The production fallback was regenerated from the real scene.

Build, typecheck and changed-file lint passed. Four requested views were visually inspected; energy motion, offscreen pause, reduced-motion demand rendering and one mocked form submission passed with no page errors. No real message, Lighthouse or broader site QA.
