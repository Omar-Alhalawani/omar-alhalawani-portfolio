# Visual direction

## Art direction: the living wafer
A substantial folded silicon form floats in a quiet navy field. Its surface contains broad cyan and violet energy ribbons, deep shadow, and a restrained magenta reflection. It is neither a sphere nor a boiling blob. Its broad top plane and layered silhouette suggest a manufactured material even before straight edges appear.

The same topology becomes a rounded die. Traces gradually reveal across the surface. Four supporting plates emerge beneath it to form five conceptual engineering layers. The camera approaches the structure gently; it never orbits freely. Subsequent scenes repeat the same oblique projection, material palette, trace width, and signal vocabulary.

## Composition
Editorial typography on the left; a large uninterrupted object on the right, with substantial surrounding space. Desktop hero artwork occupies about 55% of the field. Text sits on opaque negative space. On phones, copy appears first and the constrained object sits below it. No card wall in the hero, fake CPU meter, floating icon cloud, or terminal aesthetic.

## Surface language
Near-black background, slightly warmer off-white type, hairline slate borders, subtle stepped geometry. Cyan describes signal activity; violet describes underlying material. Other accents are exceptional. Project rows alternate restrained evidence and conceptual graphics rather than duplicating generic SaaS cards.

## Photography
Omar's portrait belongs on About. Actual project photos belong in case-study evidence. The home signature remains the custom generated WebGL form, not unrelated stock art.
