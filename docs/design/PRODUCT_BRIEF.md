# Product brief

## Outcome
An independent, non-indexable staging portfolio for Omar Alhalawani. Within the first viewport, a technical recruiter must see his name, Computer Systems Engineering at Carleton, ASIC verification, and his Summer 2026 Ciena co-op. Resume, projects, and contact are always one navigation action away.

## Identity
**OA // SYSTEMS**. The visual thesis is **silicon → signal → system**: one living piece of computational material gradually becomes an engineered structure. The metaphor is conceptual, never a company architecture or literal semiconductor fabrication stack.

## Audience and priority
1. Semiconductor recruiters: identity, Ciena, FIFO, resume.
2. Engineers: actual interfaces, design decisions, verification, source.
3. Systems collaborators: instrumentation, QNX, embedded work, contact.

## Boundaries
V1 is read-only. All implementation is in `Omar-Alhalawani/omar-portfolio-v2`. No DNS changes, purchases, paid services, proprietary information, or FAULT//HUNT. No fabricated coverage, benchmarks, test counts, or finished-project claims.

## Acceptance
Static HTML contains every essential fact. A coherent custom shader hero, gradual geometry morph, five engineering layers, verification pipeline, interactive project floorplan, and source-backed case studies are progressive enhancements. Normal scrolling, accessible links and forms, reduced motion, WebGL failure, and narrow mobile layouts must all work. Vercel staging must be verified in a real browser before it is called complete.
